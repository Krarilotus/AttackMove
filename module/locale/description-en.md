# Attack Move

**Author**: Monsterfish
**Version**: 1.0.1

Makes the game's hidden "attack move" reliable and lets you move onto troops with Alt. Every part
can be switched off in the settings.

## Attack move (Shift waypoints)

Select troops, hold **Shift** and click on the map. Every click adds a waypoint (up to nine),
and the troops walk through them in order. On the way they follow their stance:

- **Defensive** or **aggressive**: archers, crossbowmen and other ranged troops stop and
  shoot at enemies that come into range, melee troops go for enemies that come close
  (further in the aggressive stance). When the enemy is dealt with they carry on along the
  route.
- **Stand ground**: they just walk the route.

In the unmodified game this only worked right after selecting the troops: once they had
walked anywhere, Shift-clicks did nothing until you selected them again. Now Shift-clicking
works at any time:

- If the troops are still walking a Shift route, a Shift-click adds a waypoint to it.
- If they are still walking to the spot of a normal move order, that spot becomes the first
  waypoint and your Shift-click the next one (can be switched off).
- Otherwise the first Shift-click starts a new route from where they stand.

## Moving with Alt

With troops selected, hold **Alt** and click:

- on another group of your troops: the selected troops walk there instead of the click
  selecting the other troops. That makes it easy to put troops on top of each other.
- on an enemy: the selected troops walk to that spot instead of attacking that one enemy.
  Send melee troops right into an enemy crowd this way and they fight whatever is around
  them once they get there.

Without Alt, clicking works as before.

Works in both Stronghold Crusader and Stronghold Crusader Extreme.
